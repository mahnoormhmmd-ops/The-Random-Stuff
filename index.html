Noodles

Quick, cheap, and delicious... but here's what's going on inside that tasty bowl.

🧂

Loads of Salt

One packet can hold a big chunk of the salt you should have in a whole day, which is hard on your blood pressure.

🛢️

Hidden Fats

Many instant noodles are fried before they're packed, which adds extra calories and saturated fat.

❤️

Long-term Risks

Some studies link eating them very often leads to a higher risk of heart, reproductive and metabolic problems.

🍞

Not much Nutrition

Make from refined flour, so theres little fiber and protien. You feel hungry veryy soon so you eat again and again.

Not everything is bad news: add an egg and some veggies, and use only half the flavor packet. It makes a big difference! (Gotta eat anyway, so maybe like this.)

Brownies

Warm, gooey, and impossible to say no to... but here's what that harmless little cuboid is really doing.

🍬

Sugary Overload

One brownie can hold more sugar than you need in a whole day, which is rough on your teeth and your energy levels.

🧈

Butter and fat

Rich brownies are made with lots of butter, so they're high in saturated fat and calories for a small piece.

📉

The sugar crash

Your blood sugar shoots up fast, then drops, which leaves you tired and craving another snack.

🦷

Hard on teeth

Sticky sugar clings to your teeth and feeds the bacteria that cause cavities.

Not all bad news: go for a smaller square, share it, or pick dark chocolate.
